# SunPower Solutions — 3 to 5 Minute Mentor & Judge Demo Script

## Demo Strategy & Setup
- **Target Audience**: Hackathon Judges, Product Mentors, Solar Industry Experts.
- **Goal**: Demonstrate how SunPower Solutions eliminates paper trails and spreadsheets by executing the full digital lifecycle in under 5 minutes.
- **Live Demo Link**: [https://hakathonodoo.vercel.app/](https://hakathonodoo.vercel.app/)

---

## Step-by-Step Presentation Script

### 1. Introduction (0:00 – 0:30)
- **Visual**: Open [https://hakathonodoo.vercel.app/](https://hakathonodoo.vercel.app/) (Landing Page).
- **Speaker Pitch**:
  > "Hello judges! Rooftop solar in India is booming with the PM Surya Ghar initiative, but installers still struggle with fragmented tools—leads in WhatsApp, quotes in Excel, serial numbers on paper, and warranty claims lost in email.
  > Today we present **SunPower Solutions**—a complete enterprise solar platform managing every step from Lead to 5-Year Warranty."
- **Action**: Show the interactive Solar Savings Calculator on the landing page, enter a ₹4,500 monthly bill, and click **"Get Solar Score"**.

---

### 2. Lead CRM & Site Survey (0:30 – 1:15)
- **Visual**: Navigate to `/leads`.
- **Speaker Pitch**:
  > "Our CRM automatically scores leads using satellite irradiance and roof data. Here is Sanjay Verma in Hyderabad with a Solar Score of 92."
- **Action**: Open Sanjay Verma's record. Show the technical survey details:
  - Shadow-free roof area: 420 sq. ft.
  - Recommended system: 5 kW On-Grid.
  - DISCOM: TSSPDEL (Sanctioned Load: 5 kW).
- **Action**: Click **"Generate Quote"**.

---

### 3. Automated Quote & PM Surya Ghar Subsidy (1:15 – 1:55)
- **Visual**: Navigate to `/quotes`.
- **Speaker Pitch**:
  > "The quote builder automatically compiles the Bill of Materials—Tier-1 Monocrystalline panels, hybrid inverter, and mounting structures.
  > Notice the real-time financial transparency:
  > - Gross system price: ₹2,45,000
  > - Direct MNRE / PM Surya Ghar subsidy: ₹78,000
  > - Customer net payable: ₹1,67,000
  > - Profit margin: 27.5%."
- **Action**: Click **"Approve & Create Sales Order"**.

---

### 4. Warehouse Dispatch & Serial Number Barcode Scan (1:55 – 2:35)
- **Visual**: Navigate to `/dispatch`.
- **Speaker Pitch**:
  > "In the warehouse, error prevention is critical. Panels must match warranty claims.
  > The warehouse manager scans component barcodes directly into the dispatch manifest. 16 solar panels and 1 string inverter are validated and locked to truck TS-09-UB-4421."
- **Action**: Show the scanned serial list (`SP-MONO-540-08912` through `SP-MONO-540-08927`).
- **Action**: Click **"Dispatch to Site"**.

---

### 5. Field Technician Mobile PWA & Customer Sign-Off (2:35 – 3:30)
- **Visual**: Switch to `/jobs` or `/technician` (simulating mobile view).
- **Speaker Pitch**:
  > "On site, our technician uses our offline-first PWA. No clipboard needed:
  > 1. GPS coordinates and inverter commissioning telemetry are captured automatically.
  > 2. Proof-of-installation photos are uploaded with timestamp watermarks.
  > 3. The customer signs directly on the technician's touchscreen."
- **Action**: Demonstrate the digital signature canvas and click **"Complete Installation"**.

---

### 6. Automatic 5-Year Warranty & Service Ticket (3:30 – 4:10)
- **Visual**: Navigate to `/warranties` and `/tickets`.
- **Speaker Pitch**:
  > "The moment the job is signed off, the system automatically generates an active 5-year warranty policy with certificate `SUN-WAR-2026-0042`.
  > If the customer ever experiences a generation drop, our SLA ticketing system immediately alerts the service coordinator."
- **Action**: Show the generated warranty card with serial numbers linked.

---

### 7. Director Executive Dashboard & Real Data (4:10 – 5:00)
- **Visual**: Open `/dashboard`.
- **Speaker Pitch**:
  > "Finally, the Director Executive Dashboard gives leadership real-time visibility into the business:
  > - Monthly Revenue: ₹24.50 Lakh
  > - Gross Profit: ₹6.70 Lakh (27.3% Margin)
  > - Warehouse Valuation: ₹42.50 Lakh
  > - 54.2 Tonnes of CO₂ saved
  > Every single calculation is protected against missing data, and solar benchmarks are validated against live NASA POWER satellite feeds.
  > Thank you!"
